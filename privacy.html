Read Along Uploader

Read Along Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Read Along English Stories YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own easy-English story videos to that channel and sets their titles, descriptions and schedules.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What data the tool uses

The tool uses Google sign-in and the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and set video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, schedule) on the channel owner's own YouTube channel.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • We do not access any YouTube account other than the owner's own channel.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored privately and used only to upload videos to the owner's channel. Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Videos are uploaded under the YouTube Terms of Service.
